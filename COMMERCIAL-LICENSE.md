## Free: noncommercial use

You do not need a commercial license if you use the packages:

- for personal projects that earn no money;

- in noncommercial open-source projects and mods;

## Paid: commercial use

You need a commercial license if the engine is part of anything that earns money or
supports a business, directly or indirectly. For example:

- a game or app that is sold, including early access;
- a free game or app with ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid content;
- a paid service, including a hosted battle server or any software offered as a service;
- products, tools or internal systems of a for-profit company;
- work for a client, for example a contractor building a product with the engine;
- a project funded by donations, crowdfunding or sponsorship that sells or monetizes
  the result.

If you are not sure whether your case is commercial, write to us before you release.

## What the commercial license gives you

- The right to use the packages in commercial products and services.
- The right to modify the packages and distribute them inside your product, for
  example in a bundled web app or a game build, without publishing your source code.
- No noncommercial restrictions for the licensed product or company.

The scope, term, price and any support are agreed individually. Common options are a
license per product, a license per company, or a license tied to revenue.

## How to get a license

Email mdpopov11@gmail.com and tell us:

- who you are: a person or a company, and the country;
- what you are building and how it earns money;
- which `@heroes2dgame/*` packages you use;
- the expected scale, for example audience or revenue.

We reply with terms and a draft agreement.
